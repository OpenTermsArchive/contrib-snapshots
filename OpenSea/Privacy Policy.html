Feedback. If you provide us with feedback or contact us, we may receive your name and contact information (such as an email address), as well as any other content included in or associated with the message you send.

Promotions and Rewards. If you participate in a contest, rewards program, promotion, or sweepstakes, we may collect your wallet address, name, contact information, and other details relevant to that program, and, where needed for tax reporting, your Social Security number, employer identification number, or other taxpayer identification number and your address (for example, on an IRS Form W-9 or W-8BEN). B. Information Collected Automatically

As you navigate through and interact with the Platform and Services, we may use automatic data collection technologies to collect certain information, including:

Interactions with the Platform and Services. To provide and improve the Platform and Services, analyze trends, and enforce the Terms, we collect information when you interact with the Platform and Services, including, but not limited to, your browser/device type, device identifiers (such as device fingerprint and device ID), operating system and device settings, IP address and associated geolocation, blockchain wallet address, wallet type, actions and clickstream data, referring/exit pages, and date/time stamps. We may also store this data in log files. We use device fingerprinting only to provide security, to detect and prevent fraud, and to comply with legal obligations (such as restricting access from certain jurisdictions).

Cookies and Tracking Technologies. When you use the Platform and Services, we and our third-party partners use cookies, pixel tags, and similar technologies to collect information about your activity, browser, and device. This section describes what these technologies are, how we use them, and your choices. Third-party providers listed in this section may collect information about your online activities over time and across different websites when you use the Platform and Services.

Cookies are small text files stored on your browser or device when you visit a website. They may be “session” cookies (deleted when you close your browser) or “persistent” cookies (stored until they expire or you delete them). We also use pixel tags (sometimes called web beacons or clear GIFs), which are small code snippets that allow us to understand how users interact with the Platform and Services.

We organize cookies into four categories. These categories correspond to the choices presented in the cookie preferences panel.

Strictly Necessary Cookies. These cookies are essential for the Platform and Services to function and cannot be disabled. They include cookies that maintain your authenticated session, store your consent preferences, provide security and fraud protection, and support infrastructure services such as bot management and content delivery.

Functional Cookies. These cookies remember your preferences and settings to provide a more personalized experience. They include your language and currency preferences, user interface layout choices (such as sidebar states, table views, and filter panel positions), and display settings. These cookies do not track your activity across other websites.

Analytics Cookies. These cookies help us understand how visitors use the Platform and Services, measure performance, and improve the user experience. The following third-party providers set analytics cookies:

ProviderPurpose
AmplitudeMeasures how users interact with the Platform and Services (session and device identifiers)
DatadogMeasures page load times and application performance
Google Analytics (GA4)Traffic measurement and audience analytics

Marketing Cookies. These cookies support attribution and referral tracking. The following providers set marketing cookies:

ProviderPurpose
AmplitudeMarketing attribution (tracks how you arrived at the Platform)
OpenSeaReferral code capture (records referral source when you arrive via a referral link)

Cookie Durations

Strictly necessary cookies persist for the duration of your session or as long as needed for their security or authentication function (ranging from 30 minutes for bot management to 30 days for session refresh). Functional, analytics, and marketing cookies persist for up to one year. You can clear cookies at any time through your browser settings or the cookie preferences panel.

How Consent Works

We apply different consent models depending on your location:

Opt-in regions (European Union, United Kingdom, Liechtenstein, Iceland, Norway, Switzerland, Canada, and Brazil): Analytics, marketing, and functional cookies are blocked — and the associated tracking scripts are not downloaded — until you affirmatively grant consent through the cookie banner.

All other regions: Analytics, marketing, and functional cookies are active by default. You may opt out at any time through the cookie preferences panel.

Your consent choices are stored in a cookie on your device and respected on each subsequent visit.

Browser Privacy Signals. We do not respond to browser “Do Not Track” signals. We honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals. When your browser sends a GPC signal, we automatically disable marketing cookies for your session. You do not need to take any additional action.
