What GAMSTOP is and why these sites sit outside it

GAMSTOP is the UK's free self-exclusion scheme. Once you register, every operator holding a UK Gambling Commission licence must block your account for the period you selected, from six months up to a permanent exclusion. Casinos not on GamStop hold licences issued elsewhere, most commonly in Curaçao, so they have no obligation to connect to the UK scheme. That is a licensing difference rather than a way around any law — it simply means a different regulator sits behind the site, with its own rules on disputes, fund protection and advertising standards.
