4. How do we process your personal data?

Note: Unless otherwise specified, your data will remain within the EU and will not be passed on to third parties.

4.1 Applications

What data will be processed and how does it reach Hetzner?

The type of data that we process may change a bit depending on the job you are applying for and its requirements. We process: basic personal information, career-conditions (e.g. notice period), education and work experience, professional knowledge, attached documents / attachments, if applicable, special categories of data (e.g. if you have a disability or special needs), information about your interview and notes on it, and additional information shared with us during the application process.
We will obtain this data directly from you or – if a recruitment agency is involved – directly from the agency.

Why does Hetzner process my data and what is the legal basis for it?
  • Conclusion of an employment contract: Reviewing and processing your application. The goal is to sign an employment contract. (Art. 6 para. 1 lit. b GDPR)
  • Ensuring your protection rights: Processing special categories of personal data to protect your legal rights. (Art. 9 para. 2 lit. b GDPR in connection with Art. 6 para. 1 lit. b GDPR)
  • Inclusion in our talent pool: Storage of your documents for future positions in the company on the basis of your voluntary consent (Art. 6 para. 1 lit. a GDPR). You can revoke your permission at any time by reaching out to our personnel team at: career@hetzner.com
  • Legal documentation: Storing the data to protect against potential legal claims or to assert them if necessary. (Art. 6 para. 1 lit. f GDPR)
How will my application data be managed?

All applications are entered into our applicant management system. If you applied by post, we will digitize your documents and return the originals to you.
To process your application, a candidate profile will be created in the system. You will not have access to this profile.

Who will have access to my data and will it be sent to any third parties?

We use external software from Rexx for the application management system. This company acts as a service provider for us. They may access your data as part of maintaining the systems. We have concluded a data processing agreement (DPA) with this provider, which ensures that the data is processed only according to our instructions and in compliance with applicable data protection regulations.
You can find more information in Rexx’s (Rexx Systems GmbH, Süderstrasse 75-79, 20097 Hamburg, Germany) privacy policy: https://www.rexx-systems.com/datenschutz/.
If your application does not match a position at Hetzner Online, we may check whether another company in our group (Hetzner Cloud GmbH, Hetzner Finland Oy) might be interested in your profile and whether sharing your application would benefit your career opportunities. Your documents will only be shared if the position is equivalent or similar to the one you applied for. (Art. 6 para. 1 lit. f GDPR)
If you do not agree to this, you can object at any time. Please contact our HR team: career@hetzner.com.

How long will my data be saved?

The length of time your data will be stored can vary depending on the results of your application process:

  • Rejection or withdrawal of the application: Six months after the rejection or withdrawal of your application.
  • Storage in our talent pool: 24 months. You can revoke your permission for this at any time. Please contact our HR team to do so: career@hetzner.com
  • You become an employee: Your personal data will become part of your employee file.

Once the deadlines are reached, we will delete your data.

4.5 AI

4.5.1 Ask AI chatbot

An AI chatbot is integrated into this website. It can answer general questions about our products.
If you have questions about your customer account, please contact us via https://accounts.hetzner.com.

What data do we process and how does it reach Hetzner?

Chatbot
We process the information that you enter in the chat. You do not need to provide any personal data to use the chatbot.

Your request is first sent to Hetzner and automatically checked for personal data (PII tool). Any personal data that is detected is removed before the request is sent to our external AI service. The external AI service then creates an answer and sends it back to Hetzner. If necessary, we add the previously removed personal data back to the answer. The answer is then shown to you in the chat.

If the chatbot cannot answer your question, you can send us a support request. In this case, we process:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Customer number
  • Chat ID
  • AI-generated summary of your problem

For more information, please see the section “Customer inquiries”.

Cookies
We use the cookies that are required for the general operation of our website. For more information, please see the section “Website”.

Logfiles
We collect the logfiles that are required for the general operation of our website. For more information, please see the section “Website”.

In addition, we assign an internal identifier (request ID) to each request and record whether the request was processed successfully (status and error messages).

The external AI service also processes technical data that is required for the operation, security and monitoring of the service.

Why do we process the data and what is the legal basis?

We process the content of your request to answer your questions. (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR)

We process technical data to ensure the secure and reliable operation of the chatbot. (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR)

Who has access to the data and is it shared with third parties?

Your data is only accessible to authorized Hetzner employees.

We use an external AI service with servers located in the EU to provide the AI function. The provider only receives the previously cleaned request without personal data. The service provider processes the data only according to our instructions. We have concluded a contract with the provider to ensure compliance with data protection requirements.

Your requests are not used to train or improve AI models.

How long do we store the data?
  • Chat histories and related logfiles: 3 years (Sections 195, 199 German Civil Code (BGB))
  • Logfiles at our external AI service: up to 90 days
  • If you open a support ticket: Please see the section “Customer inquiries”.
4.5.2 Inference

With Inference, we provide AI models that you can integrate into your applications via an application programming interface (API). The AI models are operated entirely on our own Hetzner infrastructure.

What data do we process and how does it reach Hetzner?

Prompts and outputs
The content of your request (prompt) is sent to our Hetzner servers and processed there to generate an answer (output).

Logfiles
When you use the API, we also store the following technical data in a logfile:

  • IP address (we store this in anonymized form)
  • Date and time of access
  • Metadata of the API request (including HTTP requests and status codes)
  • Settings selected by the user for the API request (e.g. the maximum length of the generated answer)

The content of your requests and answers is not included in the logfiles.

Billing data
A Hetzner customer account is required to use Inference. The use of the AI service is billed via this account. In this context, we store the following billing data:

  • Project ID
  • Name of the API token (you can choose this yourself)
  • Time of the request
  • Number of input and output tokens processed

The content of your requests and answers is not included in the billing data.

Why do we process the data and what is the legal basis?

We process the content of your requests and the billing data to provide the Inference service and to bill you for its use. (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR)

We process the logfiles to ensure the secure and reliable operation of the service. (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR)

Who has access to the data and is it shared with third parties?
  • Your requests and the answers are processed on our servers and do not leave the Hetzner infrastructure.
  • Only authorized Hetzner employees have access to the billing data and logfiles.
How long do we store the data?
  • Prompts and outputs: Under our zero-retention policy, we do not store them. They are also not used in any other way to train or improve the AI models.
  • Logfiles: 6 months
  • Billing data: 8 years (in accordance with statutory tax and commercial law retention requirements)

4.6.1 Verification of customer information

4.6.1.1 New customers

To minimize our business risk and prevent fraud, we carry out a risk assessment when new customer accounts are registered.

What data do we process and how does it reach Hetzner?

Based on defined criteria and our experience, we assess how likely it is that you will meet your contractual payment obligations and use our services responsibly.

In particular, we consider the registration data you provide, technical characteristics and, where applicable, information from existing or previous contractual relationships. We also use an external service provider to support the risk assessment.

Why do we process the data and what is the legal basis?

We process your data to minimize our business risk and to prevent fraud. (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR)

Who has access to the data and is it shared with third parties?

Our employees responsible for this process have access to the data.

We also use an external service provider to support the risk assessment. For security reasons, we do not provide information about the specific assessment criteria or how they are weighted, as this could make it easier for users to create fake accounts or misuse our services.

How long do we store your data?

The result of the assessment is stored in your customer account for the duration of the customer relationship.

For information about the retention and deletion periods for customer accounts, please see the section "Customer account".

Further information about the risk assessment

The result of the risk assessment is considered as one of several factors in the decision. The final decision on whether a customer account is activated is always made by one of our employees responsible for this process and is therefore not based solely on automated processing.

For security reasons, we do not provide specific details about why a verification was unsuccessful. We also do not disclose the underlying logic of the process, as this could make it easier for users to create fake accounts or misuse our services.

If you would like us to review a decision made in connection with the risk assessment, you can contact cda-review@hetzner.com.

4.6.1.2 Existing customers

To prevent payment defaults and fraud and to protect our services, we carry out an ongoing risk assessment during the customer relationship based on defined criteria and our experience.

What data do we process and how does it reach Hetzner?

In particular, we process information about the existing business relationship, such as previous payment behavior, orders and previous use of our products and services.
When reviewing individual orders, we may also ask you to provide information about how you plan to use our products.

Why do we process the data and what is the legal basis?

We process this data to prevent payment defaults, to prevent fraud and to protect our services. (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR)

Who has access to the data and is it shared with third parties?

Our employees responsible for this process have access to the data. The data is not shared with third parties.

How long do we store your data?

The result of the assessment is stored in your customer account for the duration of the customer relationship and is continuously updated. For information about the retention and deletion periods for customer accounts, please see the section "Customer account".

We store information that you provide to us as part of an order review for three years. (Sections 195, 199 German Civil Code (BGB))

Further information about the risk assessment

The final decision about which products or services can be ordered, which order or usage limits apply and which functions are available within the customer account is always made by one of our employees responsible for this process and is therefore not based solely on automated processing.

4.6.1.3 Identity verification

What data do we process and how does it reach Hetzner?
  • Identity verification by Hetzner: From the identity document you provide, we receive the following information: name, address and expiry date of the document. Other information in the identity document can be blacked out.
  • Identity verification by iDenfy: For information about the data collected by iDenfy, please see the company's privacy policy: iDenfy UAB (K. Baršausko g. 59, LT-51423 Kaunas, Lithuania): https://www.idenfy.com/privacy-policy/. We receive the verification result afterwards.
Why do we process the data and what is the legal basis?

Identity verification is carried out to prevent fraud and payment defaults and to protect our services against misuse. (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR)

In addition, the verification is carried out to perform the contract, as you are required to provide proof that your information is correct if we request it. (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR in conjunction with section 2.3 of our Terms and Conditions)

Who has access to the data and is it shared with third parties?
  • Identity verification by Hetzner: Only our employees responsible for this process have access to the data. The data is not shared with third parties.
  • Identity verification by iDenfy: We send your name and the country where you live to iDenfy. However, the data is only transferred when you click the verification link.
How long do we store your data?
  • Identity verification by Hetzner: Your data is deleted immediately after the verification.
  • Identity verification by iDenfy: The provider stores your data for two months. An individual exception to this automated deletion and retention process is currently not possible. This also applies if no customer relationship is established after the identity verification has been completed.

4.6.4 Verification of domain holder data

Due to legal cybersecurity requirements, DENIC eG, as the central registry for .de domains, is required to ensure that correct and reachable holder data is available for every .de domain.

If DENIC has doubts about the accuracy or validity of the holder data stored for your domain, we as the registrar are required to verify this information.

What data do we process and how does it reach Hetzner?

For private individuals, we require proof of your identity and address to verify your holder data. You can provide this proof, for example, with an identity document, a registration certificate or a utility bill.

For companies, we also require additional proof of the company, for example an extract from the commercial register.

The documents are provided by you directly to Hetzner after we have asked you to verify the data.

Why do we process the data and what is the legal basis?

We process the data to verify the accuracy of the holder data provided when the domain was registered and to send the result of the verification to DENIC.

We process the data to comply with a legal obligation. (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR in conjunction with the legal requirements for the verification of domain holder data and the related requirements of DENIC.)

Who has access to the data and is it shared with third parties?

Only the Hetzner employees responsible for the verification have access to the documents.

We only send the result of the verification and, where applicable, updated holder data to DENIC.

The documents you provide as proof are not shared with DENIC.

How long do we store the data?

The documents you provide as proof are deleted immediately after the verification has been completed.

Further information

Hetzner has no influence on DENIC's review and cannot provide information about why or which data was marked as invalid.

If the required documents are not provided or the holder data cannot be confirmed, DENIC may take measures up to and including deactivation and deletion of the domain.

We transfer the data to enforce our payment claims. (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR)

Who has access to the data and is it shared with third parties?

At Hetzner, only authorized Hetzner employees have access.

To collect outstanding amounts, we transfer the required data to Intrum. Intrum processes the transferred data under its own responsibility. For more information, please see Intrum's privacy information: https://www.intrum.de/zahlungserinnerung-erhalten/

How long do we store the data?

We store the data held by us for eight years due to statutory retention requirements. (Section 257 German Commercial Code (HGB), Section 147 German Fiscal Code (AO))

4.7 Newsletter

In our newsletter, we inform you about new products and current topics.

1. Newsletter registration
What data do we process and how does it reach Hetzner?

When you register for our newsletter, you provide us with your email address. You can also voluntarily provide your name and job profile when you register.
We store the date of registration in the background. In addition, your browser automatically sends us your IP address.

Why do we process the data and what is the legal basis?

Registration for the newsletter is voluntary. After you register, we send you a double opt-in link. You use this link to confirm that you want to receive our newsletter in the future. This means that your registration is only successfully completed after you click the link in the confirmation email. (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR)

How long do we store the data?

You must confirm that you want to receive the newsletter within 30 days. If you do not confirm within this period, we delete your data.

If you confirm that you want to receive the newsletter, we store your data, including the double opt-in, until you unsubscribe from the newsletter. For more information, please see the section "Unsubscribing from the newsletter".

2. Sending the newsletter
What data do we process and how does it reach Hetzner?

When we send our newsletter, we store the complete content of the email, including the subject, the recipient's email address and the recipient's name (email preview).
We also store technical events, such as the status of the email (e.g. "sent") and error messages.

Why do we process the data and what is the legal basis?

We store this data to ensure that our newsletter is sent correctly. (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR)

How long do we store the data?

We store the data for 24 months and then delete it.

3. Tracking

5. What rights do I have when it comes to my data?

The GDPR grants you the following rights regarding your data:

  • Right of access:
    You can request information at any time about whether and which personal data we process about you.
  • Right to rectification:
    If the data we have stored about you is incorrect or incomplete, you can request its correction or completion.
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten"):
    You can request the deletion of your data if it is no longer required for its original purpose. However, please note that immediate deletion may not be possible in some cases due to legal or contractual retention obligations.
  • Right to restriction of processing:
    If, for example, you dispute the accuracy of your data or believe that the processing is unlawful, you can request that we restrict the processing of your data.
  • Right to data portability:
    You have the right to receive your data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format. You can also request that we transfer the data directly to a third party, if technically feasible.
  • Right to object:
    You can object to the processing of your personal data unless there are compelling legitimate reasons for the processing.
  • Right not to be subject to automated decision-making:
    We do not make any automated decisions regarding your person. If we did, you would have the right not to be evaluated solely by algorithms.

If you would like to make use of any of these rights, please contact our data protection officer directly.
